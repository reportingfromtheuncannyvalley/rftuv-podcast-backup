Noel: A report from Palo Alto Networks, covered by SiliconANGLE on November sixteenth, found that organizations broadly struggle to track and secure the A I agents running in their cloud infrastructure. The gap between what security teams think they have deployed and what is actually running is not a rounding error. According to Techzine Global, ninety nine percent of organizations report finding A I agents in places where they were not expected. That figure is Techzine reporting the survey. It means virtually every organization that goes looking finds agents it did not plan for, did not authorize, or simply lost track of between deployment and production.

Noel: Ellis lays out why this is structural. Agents get deployed fast, often by individual teams or developers working outside central I T. They connect to services, accumulate permissions, and handle data. Then a team moves on, the original use case shifts, and the agent keeps running. Nobody files a ticket to turn it off. According to Security Info Watch, ungoverned agents are already inside clients' buildings right now, in production, not in a laboratory warning about some future risk. An ungoverned agent is not necessarily malicious. It may have been built for a legitimate purpose and simply drifted out of anyone's active oversight. But it still makes decisions, still accesses resources, and still acts with whatever credentials it was given. If those credentials were never reviewed or properly limited, the agent operates with more trust than any security team would grant it.

Noel: The market has read that gap, and it moved the next day. Pretxt launched on November seventeenth, announcing a runtime oversight layer designed to supervise A I agents in real time. SiliconANGLE reported the launch came with five million dollars in funding. Runtime is the moment an agent is actually executing, making decisions, and handling data, which is different from the checks applied before deployment. An agent can pass every review on the way in and still behave unexpectedly once running. Watching it as it acts gives a team the chance to intervene before consequences compound, instead of auditing a log after the fact. Five million dollars is an early stage bet, and Pretxt is one company. Visibility comes before every other control. You cannot govern what you cannot find, and right now, by these numbers, most organizations cannot find all of it.

Noel: Second story, still national. A cascade of technology outages has run for days, and the Secretary of State has now addressed it directly. Speaking to reporters in Manila on Tuesday, while traveling with President Donald Trump, Secretary of State Marco Rubio said there is, in his word, zero information that the outages are related to each other or caused by a malign actor. He said there is zero evidence or any reason to believe that any of these isolated instances have anything to do with nefarious actors, cyber hackers, criminals, another nation state, or a malign actor of any kind. Keep each failure separate, because that is how the reporting presents them. The sequence began Thursday, when Microsoft email and other tools went down, affecting millions of workers, and Microsoft traced the issue to an earlier network capacity upgrade. Flights across the country were grounded on Friday, in the longest ground stop since September eleventh, and the Federal Aviation Administration blamed a router upgrade. By Monday, customers reported difficulty reaching online accounts at several major banks and the money transfer service Zelle, after a technical issue at JPMorgan Chase rippled through the financial system. Chase said its problem was a software bug and was not cyber related. On Tuesday, an internal network the National Security Agency uses to communicate classified information was offline after what the agency described as routine maintenance. Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton said the government is not seeing this as a cyberattack incident, and that the incidents were caused by security upgrades that took longer than planned or hit unexpected problems. That is the official account. Separate systems, separate stated causes, and no evidence offered of a link.
