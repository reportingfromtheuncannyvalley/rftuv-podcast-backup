Closer to home, there's a house now open in Montrose with a very practical purpose: giving veterans' families somewhere to stay while someone they love receives care.

It's the Fisher House at VA Hudson Valley Health Care, on the Franklin Delano Roosevelt Hospital campus.

The house was dedicated on September thirtieth by Medical Center Director Dawn M. Schaal and Fisher House President David Coker. The Fisher House Foundation built it and gifted it to the VA.

Inside its eleven thousand four hundred square feet are twelve wheelchair-accessible suites, a spacious kitchen, shared living, dining and family rooms, and a laundry room. There's a patio, too.

Lodging is free for families of veterans receiving medical care, with a particular welcome for families of veterans in inpatient mental health care and residential rehabilitation treatment.

It's New York's fourth Fisher House, joining one in Albany and two in the Bronx.

The rooms and the kitchen serve a simple purpose. A veteran's family can stay beside the hospital instead of driving home from it. There's a place to make a meal, do the laundry, and spend the night nearby while treatment continues.
