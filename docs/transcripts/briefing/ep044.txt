Our third story is national, reflecting a dynamic Hudson Valley listeners have been following this cycle.

A Politico poll conducted October sixteenth through nineteen, reported October twenty fifth, found fifty percent of Americans now oppose a data center within three miles of their home. In January that number was twenty eight percent. Only twenty one percent said they support one nearby.

The Associated Press reported October twenty second, by Marc Levy and Kaitlyn Huamani, that technology companies facing community opposition are promising fewer taxpayer handouts, more transparency, and more money to host communities. Anti-data-center sentiment has become a significant issue in the midterms, and local opposition blocking municipal permits has become a bottleneck for the industry.

Google and Meta told AP they are working to address mistrust through earlier consultation. Amazon launched its Amazon Data Center Commitment this month, pledging better environmental ratings for backup generators and more disclosure on energy and water use. Amazon, Microsoft, and Google have said they stopped requiring government officials to sign nondisclosure agreements, and Meta is reviewing its use of such agreements. Per AP, those agreements have meant host communities often know little about a project until it reaches a municipal board.

Amazon, Google, Meta, and Microsoft project combined capital spending of more than six hundred billion dollars this year, primarily on U.S. data centers. Meta and Amazon have each pledged one billion dollars in financial incentives to host communities, and Google's local investment will reach one billion dollars by end of twenty twenty seven. President Trump urged the companies Wednesday to pay communities a dividend.

Sam Burleigh, who helped start Concerned Citizens of Montour County in Pennsylvania, called Amazon's commitments propaganda, as AP reports him. A Senate Democrats report this month criticized the companies for nondisclosure agreements, tax break requests, and not covering the full cost of the grid buildout they require.
