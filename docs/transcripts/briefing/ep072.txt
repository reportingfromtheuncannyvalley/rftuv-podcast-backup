Noel: Good evening. I am Noel Brown, and this is the R F T U V Evening Briefing for Tuesday, November seventeenth, twenty twenty-six.

Noel: We start nationally, with a warning from Google that changes the timing of a race every security team already runs. Our own Ellis Ward reported it today on R F T U V, and it is live on our site now.

Noel: On November sixteenth, Google's Threat Intelligence Group, known as GTIG, published its A I Threat Tracker under the title Agentic Threat Activity Crosses a Significant Threshold. According to Google, its researchers found an exposed staging server hosting a multi agent framework they call exploit agent. The framework connects to several large language models, some commercial and some run locally by the operator. Its job, in Google's account, is continuous differential analysis of popular web software. It compares code before and after a security patch is announced, works out what weakness the patch closed, drafts an exploit for that weakness, and then executes that exploit automatically, without human intervention.

Noel: Google says that, in the past quarter, it identified threat actors using language model frameworks to fully automate vulnerability discovery and exploitation without a human in the loop for the first time. CyberScoop, reporting on the same findings, characterized the operators as Russian speaking hackers who ran the first fully automated A I cyberattack. That word first belongs to Google and to CyberScoop. It is their characterization, based on a staging server they examined, not an independently verified record, and I am presenting it that way.

Noel: The details Google does publish are specific. Victim telemetry on the server pointed toward French websites in media, entertainment, and education. The server also held stolen victim data and scripts for work after a break in. A configuration file carried comments in Russian, one translated by Google as a note about language model A P I keys for auto exploitation. The worked example in the report shows the framework reading access control code in Spectra, a WordPress plugin with more than one million active installations.

Noel: The honest limits matter as much as the headline. Google gives no success rate for this framework, no count of victims, and no named operator or country. It does not say how long the framework took to go from a patch to a working exploit. What is established is narrower, and still large. A patch is supposed to close a hole. Here, the patch also became the map. This is distinct from the ARTEX campaign we reported on November tenth, where a known pentesting tool ran agent to agent attacks, and distinct from the A P I key campaign aimed at LiteLLM and other applications. Same broad family, different framework, different mechanism, different evidence. If you run a public website, the lesson is plain. The window between a patch and an exploit just got shorter, so patch quickly, especially on plugins.

Noel: Second story, and it is about who counts. Proposed changes to the twenty thirty census could remove more than one million people from the official count for New York and Connecticut combined. No one would move. No one would leave. They would simply stop appearing in the figures used to divide seats in Congress and to guide federal funding.

Noel: According to News Twelve Hudson Valley, and our own Mara Quinn's reporting today, the Census Bureau published the proposed rule in September. The core change would exclude non citizens who do not hold a green card from the population count used for congressional apportionment. That includes undocumented immigrants, temporary visa holders, refugees, and asylum seekers. The proposal would also remove race and ethnicity questions, and anchor residence to where a person lives between January third and April first. The administration's stated rationale, as reported, is that residents without that status lack a sufficient tie and allegiance to be counted as inhabitants for this purpose, and that the change ensures fidelity to the Constitution. News Twelve reported the administration did not respond to its request for comment on the proposal.

Noel: The numbers behind the warning come from twenty twenty-three estimates cited by News Twelve, from the Center for Migration Studies, the Pew Research Center, and the Migration Policy Institute. New York has between eight hundred twenty-five thousand and nine hundred twenty thousand undocumented residents. Connecticut has between one hundred sixty-eight thousand and one hundred ninety-eight thousand. Added together, that is well over one million people on paper.

Noel: State leaders are pushing back. Connecticut Governor Ned Lamont warned about the winter residence window, with an example of a person in Milford most of the year who spends a few months in Florida. Representative Rosa DeLauro said the census determines how trillions of dollars in federal funds are allocated, and called the proposal an effort to rig the system. New York Attorney General Letitia James said her office fought this issue in twenty nineteen, won, and is prepared to go to court again. Comments closed on November second. For the Hudson Valley, this is not abstract. A smaller count reaches representation and funding formulas for roads, schools, and Medicaid that local communities live on for a decade.

Noel: Now the local closer, and it is one every commuter between Dutchess and Ulster will use all winter. The Mid-Hudson Bridge rehabilitation has wrapped up Phase One, and the winter traffic pattern has been in effect since November thirteenth.

Noel: The Bridge Authority announced the wind down on November twelfth. This is a thirty-four point four million dollar rehabilitation, on schedule for substantial completion in late twenty twenty-seven, with Phase Two in March twenty twenty-seven. Here is the pattern, exactly. At all times, eastbound traffic heading toward Route Nine south uses the far right lane only. In the morning peak, from six to nine, there are two eastbound lanes toward Poughkeepsie and one westbound lane toward Highland. In the afternoon peak, from three to six, that flips, with two westbound lanes toward Highland and one eastbound lane toward Poughkeepsie. Off peak, from nine in the morning to three in the afternoon and from six in the evening to six in the morning, there is one lane each way. Plan for that flip at rush hour, and give yourself extra time while traffic settles into it.
