Silas: It is worth settling another identification before we proceed. A mastodon was not a mammoth.

Silas: Mastodon molars have cone-shaped cusps suited to crushing branches, leaves, and woody vegetation. This was a browser, equipped for forested surroundings.

Silas: Mammoth molars are broad and ridged, suited to grinding grasses. The distinction is not merely one large animal mistaken for another. It is a difference in the business each mouth was built to conduct.

Silas: For an animal discovered through its teeth, that is an especially useful distinction. The part that brought it to human attention is also the part that tells us something about how it lived.
